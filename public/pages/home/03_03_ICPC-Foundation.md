## Part of the International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC)

<div id="icpc-foundation" class="flex-container">
    <div class="text-container" style="padding-right: 20px;">
        <p>
            The <a href="https://icpc.global/">International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC)</a> is a global competitive programming event for university students and one of the oldest and most prestigious coding competitions, founded in 1977.
        </p>
        <p>
            The <a href="https://icpc.foundation/">ICPC Foundation</a> is a 501(c)(3) organization founded to advance the art and science of competitive programming for the benefit of society. The ICPC International Collegiate Programming Contest operates under the auspices of the ICPC Foundation which is responsible for ICPC services, sponsorship, fundraising, and outreach.
        </p>
