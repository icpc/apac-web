## Rules

1. Wear the provided contest T-shirt as the outermost garment and the badge at all times, including during contests.
  + Contest T-shirts and badges will be distributed during the onsite registration.
  + Jackets, undershirts, or any other clothing are allowed to be worn inside the provided contest T-shirt.
  + Hats are not allowed. Note that religious headgear is allowed. If you wear a hoodie, the hood must be down all the time and not over the shirt.
2. All contestants are encouraged to wear shoes.
3. Do not touch anything on the desk in the team area before the contest starts.
4. Do not eat or drink in the contest hall except the snack area. There is a designated snack area in the contest hall with drinks and snack supply. No talk or any form of communication (even between members of the same team) in the snack area.
5. Do not bring any electronic/communication devices.
  + Laptops, tablets, keyboards, mice, cameras, calculators, digital dictionaries, USB memory sticks/cards, watches, radios, mobile phones, smartphones, earphones, headphones, pagers, and other electronic/communication devices are prohibited.
  + Electronic medical aid that has to be worn by a contestant during the contest is permitted, but such an item needs to be (privately) declared during the registration.
6. Please be considerate of other teams and compete fairly.
  + A team may be disqualified if the team is found to be cheating. Cheating involves any attempt or activity by an individual or groups (contestants and/or coach) to enhance or diminish their performance or other teams' performance by means other than actual ability related to the contest, including but not limited to efforts such as submitting a solution which is not their own, hacking (successful or not) the contest management system or other machines, dislodging extension cords, or other distracting behaviours. All teams should uphold the sportsmanship and the spirit of fair competition.
