## Team Invitation

Selected teams have been promoted to the 2026 ICPC Asia Pacific Championship in the [ICPC global system](https://icpc.global/).

The coach of selected teams have been contacted by the organizer to confirm the intention to participate and to follow up with the registration process.

## On-site Registration

On-site registration will be open from 14:00 to 17:00 on March 6. Teams are required to complete registration at the Registration Area on the third floor of the contest venue.

During registration, teams will:

* Verify their identity documents (student ID and passport or other photo ID)
* Receive competition materials, including the receipt for the registration fee
* Take official team photos

Teams are encouraged to arrive early, as the registration procedures should be completed before **17:00**.

### Identity Verification Notice

During on-site registration, our staff did not verify passports or other identification documents of the contestants. As a result, a concern has been raised through the International Technical Committee regarding the possibility of proxy participation.

On behalf of the contest organization, Co-Director Min-Zheng Shieh, who is responsible for contest operations, sincerely apologizes for this oversight. We believe that, under the requirement that contestants carry valid identification documents, all teams are able to pass identity verification.

If there are concerns regarding the identity of any team members, please report them by **March 15** via email to [Min-Zheng Shieh](mailto:mzshieh@nycu.edu.tw). We will request the team to submit a photo of each contestant holding their passport, and we will also review photos taken in the team area on the contest day. These materials will be examined by the Asia Pacific Steering Committee Secretaries to determine whether any substitution has occurred, in order to safeguard the fairness of the competition.
